What changed in October 2026

Written by Quilzo's release-notes agent from the team's raw notes, and published after a person approved it.

Chatbots on static sites. A chatbot can now go with a site when it is exported to static files, and answer in the visitor's browser from the published pages, with no model. Its answers are tested against the server's.

Three new kinds of section. Tabs, a row of quotes, and a question box that opens a chatbot with the question already asked. A hero can also show a large picture under its headline.

Quilzo watches itself. Its own audit log and public site now feed the security console. Repeated failed sign-ins, new admin tokens, weakened settings, people probing for the admin and attempts to steer a chatbot all become findings.

Fourteen frameworks. Posture checks are mapped to fourteen frameworks, including FedRAMP, the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001. When a check starts failing, a finding opens.

Talk to a person. A chatbot can hand a conversation to a person. It arrives in the inbox, with a suggested reply.

Agents that wait. Runs are kept as they go. An action marked ask-first waits for a person, and a run can be resumed or replayed from any step.

A new mark. A Q in two parts: the loop in blue, and the tail in violet crossing its gap.
