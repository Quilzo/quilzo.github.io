Grounded

Every answer links to where it came from

A question is matched against your published pages two ways, and a page only counts if it covers most of what was asked. With no model, the answer is quoted from that page. With one, every sentence it writes is checked against the passages before you see it.

Pages you exclude are never read, and drafts are never published to it.

What happens when a visitor asks

  1. It says what it is

    Every conversation says it is automated, as the EU AI Act's Article 50 asks, and a compliance check flags a published page that leaves it out.

  2. It finds the pages

    Ranked two ways, by the words themselves and by overall similarity, and refused if no page covers the question.

  3. It answers, and cites

    Quoted, or written by a model and checked sentence by sentence against what it found.

  4. It hands over

    A visitor can ask for a person. The conversation lands in your inbox, kept only as long as you set.

A person, when it matters

When a visitor asks for a person, the conversation arrives in the admin's inbox. Somebody answers there, with a suggested reply drafted from your pages if you want one, and the visitor sees it on the same page.

Questions

Can the chatbot make things up?

Without a model it cannot: it only quotes your pages. With a model, every sentence it writes is checked against the passages it found, and a sentence that fails the check is dropped before the visitor sees it.

Can a visitor talk to a person?

Yes, when the owner allows it. The visitor's conversation goes to the inbox, somebody answers it there, and the visitor sees the reply on the same page.

Is anything a visitor asks stored?

Questions to the chatbot are not stored. Only a conversation a visitor chooses to hand to a person is kept, for as long as the owner sets and at most ninety days, and the visitor is told so before they send it.

Does it need a model?

No. Without one it quotes your pages. With one, it writes an answer and every sentence is checked against the passages it found.

Can it work on a static site?

Yes. A chatbot marked for static copies goes with the site when it is exported, and answers in the visitor's browser. The one on this site does exactly that.

Can I put it on another site?

Yes, in a frame, on the sites you list and nowhere else.
