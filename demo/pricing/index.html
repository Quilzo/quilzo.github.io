Questions

Is anything held back from the free version?

No. There is one program, and nothing in it checks which licence you hold.

When do I need the commercial licence?

When you cannot comply with the AGPL, or your organisation does not allow it. Otherwise you do not.

Do I need to pay for a model?

No. Chatbots can answer without one, and agents can use a model running on your own machine.
