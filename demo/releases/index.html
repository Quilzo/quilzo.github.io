Release notes, October 2026

Raw notes, as the people who made the changes wrote them. The changelog is written from these by an agent.

  • chatbot: static copies of a site now carry the chatbot; it answers in the visitor's browser from published pages, no model. Tested against the server's answers.
  • sections: new kinds tabs, quotes and ask (a question box that opens a chatbot). Hero can show a large picture under the headline.
  • self-watch: Quilzo's audit log and public site are sources for the security console. Failed sign-ins, admin tokens, weakened settings, admin probing, chatbot injection attempts become findings.
  • frameworks: posture checks mapped to 14 frameworks incl. FedRAMP, EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001. Drift opens a finding.
  • chatbots: hand-off to a person, inbox, suggested replies.
  • agents: durable runs, ask-first approval, resume, replay.
  • brand: new mark, a Q in two parts.
