Wholesale

Minimum order is twenty metres, mixed as you like. Trade price is a third off, and we do not do sale-or-return.

What carrying it involves

Cloth comes on a roll with a swatch card and the recipe for every colour on it. The card is the thing customers pick up, so we send two: one to keep on the counter and one to lose.

We restock on a bath cycle rather than on demand, which means a colour can be four weeks away. The stock page on this site is live and is the same data we work from.

Wholesale enquiry

If you run a shop and want to carry this, tell us what you sell and roughly how much you would take.

Volume, timing, whether you want the undyed cloth too.

This reaches the three people who run Aster & Alum and nobody else. We keep enquiries for two years so we can pick up a conversation that paused, then they are deleted. We do not add you to anything.
