Commissions

Cloth dyed for a room, a garment or a stage, in lengths nobody sells by the metre.

Recent work

Indigo on heavy linen · 34 metres

Curtains for a Georgian window

Eight dips, hung to dry in the arch because nothing else in Bristol is tall enough. The lightest panel is a shade paler than the darkest and the client chose which end faced the room.

Logwood and iron on wool · 12 metres

A costume for a play about a flood

It was supposed to fade over a six-week run, and it did. The director asked for the fading in writing before we started, which is the only way we will take that job.

Madder and weld on hemp · 60 metres

Table linen for a restaurant in Ghent

Six baths over three weeks, deliberately not matched. The point was that no two tables were the same colour and the room read as one thing anyway.

All six dyes on four fibres · 96 swatches

Sample book for a fashion school

The same dyes on linen, cotton, wool and silk, mounted with the recipe under each one. They asked for the failures too, so the book has a page of them.
