This guide moved to Vaigil. Automations that watch, ask or act, canaries planted across your systems, access reviews, vendor checks and notices to people about a breach are part of Vaigil, the security team's own tools, which began as this half of Quilzo. Its guide is Automations.

Quilzo keeps what it needs to defend itself, and hands the rest of the record to whoever watches it. What protects Quilzo itself stays here and is on by default. A risky sign-in has to confirm it is its person (see Sign-ins), and the shield's playbooks respond to attacks on the admin, the site, the chatbots and the agents. Its decoy tokens are quilzo shield decoy add.

Send Vaigil what happens here

Point the stream at Vaigil's feed, and quilzo serve pushes the audit log every fifteen seconds as signed OCSF batches that Vaigil can prove complete and unchanged:

quilzo config set siem.stream.url https://vaigil.example.org/feeds/quilzo
quilzo siem stream key --json > quilzo-stream-keys.json   # give these to Vaigil
quilzo siem stream status

People stay pseudonymous in what is sent unless siem.stream.reveal is set, which takes two administrators. See the audit log, streamed as it is written.
