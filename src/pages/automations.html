Not part of Quilzo. Automations that act across your security tools and other systems belong to the security automation your organisation already runs. What Quilzo automates is its own defence, and it is on from the start.

Shield playbooks

A playbook is a response decided in advance: when a signal comes this many times within this long, take these steps, and take the next stage if it comes again. Every step is one of the shield's own actions, from a fixed list, and every protection it puts in force ends by itself. There is no script and no expression in one, and a model never runs one.

Quilzo ships its playbooks. Those that act on one source or one agent act from the start; those that reach many people at once, such as locking the whole admin, watch until two administrators turn them on.

Adaptive sign-in

A sign-in to the admin that does not look like its person, such as one from further than anybody could have travelled, through Tor, or with a session copied to another device, gets no session until its person confirms it with a passkey or a fresh single sign-on. Failed sign-ins lock out the places they come from, not the person. This is signin.adaptive, on by default; turning it off is a weaker setting, which takes two administrators. Name your offices and VPNs with quilzo geo network add, so sign-ins from them read as yours. See Sign-ins.

Decoys

A decoy is a token that looks like any other and opens nothing. Plant it where a real secret is kept, such as a CI variable or a password manager: whoever presents it has a copy of what was kept there. The decoy-touched playbook shuts them out everywhere for a day, tells the security contact where the decoy had been planted, and asks for a case in the audit log. decoy-lockdown, which also locks the admin to passkeys and single sign-on, watches until two administrators decide it should act.

quilzo shield decoy add --where "CI variable"
quilzo shield decoy list
quilzo shield decoy remove ID

The token is shown once. Protection › Shield plants and removes decoys too, under Decoys.
