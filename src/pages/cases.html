This guide moved to Vaigil. Incidents with the NIS2, GDPR, DORA, SEC, CIRCIA and HIPAA clocks they start, playbooks, and actions on other tools that a second person approves are part of Vaigil, the security team's own tools, which began as this half of Quilzo. Its guide is Cases and incidents.

Quilzo keeps what it needs to defend itself, and hands the rest of the record to whoever watches it. A shield playbook whose step is open a case writes that request into the audit log, with the title, the signal, what it was about and a summary. Vaigil's rule quilzo.shield-asked-for-a-case puts it in the finding queue there; another SIEM can do the same with the record.

Send Vaigil what happens here

Point the stream at Vaigil's feed, and quilzo serve pushes the audit log every fifteen seconds as signed OCSF batches that Vaigil can prove complete and unchanged:

quilzo config set siem.stream.url https://vaigil.example.org/feeds/quilzo
quilzo siem stream key --json > quilzo-stream-keys.json   # give these to Vaigil
quilzo siem stream status

People stay pseudonymous in what is sent unless siem.stream.reveal is set, which takes two administrators. See the audit log, streamed as it is written.
