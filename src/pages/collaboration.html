Rooms

A room is open, closed to the members it names, or direct. A closed or direct room is read only by the people in it, and room list shows only the rooms you are in and the open ones.

Erasing a message rewrites the room's file as its messages are now, so no byte of the erased words is left on disk. Copies in backups made before go when those backups are rotated out. A room opened with --retention keeps its text that long, and the admin's sweep, run by quilzo serve, takes what is older off the disk the same way. A reaction is an emoji or a short code, never words.

The studio

quilzo studio records the screen into the media library, signed in with a token. A token revoked, or suspended by the shield, stops working there from the next request, with no restart. Each recording is recorded in the audit log as the token's holder, not whoever started the studio. Sound is recorded only when Record sound too is ticked, and the studio says to ask first, because sound takes in everybody who can be heard.
