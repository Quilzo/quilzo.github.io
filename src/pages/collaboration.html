Rooms and calls are no longer part of Quilzo, nor are the AI note-taker and the frame encryption under calls. Their commands, room, call, huddle, scribe, errand and sframe, are each answered with one line saying so, and exit 2. A store that kept rooms keeps its rooms/ directory as it was, and backs up and restores with it in place, but nothing in Quilzo reads it: no retention period runs on it, and no message in it can be erased through Quilzo. quilzo posture scan reports it until somebody moves it out of the store or deletes it.

What a shared screen costs

quilzo screen cost runs a lossless codec for screen content over a synthetic code editor and prints what it sends: a key frame, a keystroke and a still screen, in bytes, and typing and idle in kilobytes a second. It measures 1920 by 1080 at thirty frames a second unless --width, --height and --fps say otherwise. At those defaults a key frame is about 90 times smaller than the raw frame, a keystroke changes one tile of 510 and costs 271 bytes, and a still screen costs 89: about 8 KB a second while typing and 3 KB idle. --json gives the same figures.

The figures are the codec's alone, with no encryption added to them. --suite is still accepted, changes nothing and says so on standard error. It is a codec for screens, not cameras: a photograph or a video playing in a window has no small palette and no unchanged tiles, so it goes out as raw pixels, and the encoder counts that rather than hiding it.

The studio

quilzo studio records the screen into the media library, signed in with a token. It listens on 127.0.0.1:8084 unless --addr says otherwise, on an origin of its own, because it runs a script and the admin does not. A token revoked, or suspended by the shield, stops working there from the next request, with no restart. Each recording is recorded in the audit log as the token's holder, not whoever started the studio. Sound is recorded only when Record sound too is ticked, and the studio says to ask first, because sound takes in everybody who can be heard. Without ffmpeg on the machine, a recording is stored without its length or a poster frame.
