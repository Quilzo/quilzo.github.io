This guide moved to Vaigil. Connectors that read other systems, feeds that push to it, files that arrive, and the Feeds and Events screens are part of Vaigil, the security team's own tools, which began as this half of Quilzo. Its guide is Connectors.

Quilzo keeps what it needs to defend itself, and hands the rest of the record to whoever watches it. Shadow AI that people across the organisation use is found in their proxy and DNS logs, which Vaigil collects. What Quilzo's own agents reached around its model gateway is still on the Fleet screen.

Send Vaigil what happens here

Point the stream at Vaigil's feed, and quilzo serve pushes the audit log every fifteen seconds as signed OCSF batches that Vaigil can prove complete and unchanged:

quilzo config set siem.stream.url https://vaigil.example.org/feeds/quilzo
quilzo siem stream key --json > quilzo-stream-keys.json   # give these to Vaigil
quilzo siem stream status

People stay pseudonymous in what is sent unless siem.stream.reveal is set, which takes two administrators. See the audit log, streamed as it is written.
