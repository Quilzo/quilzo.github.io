What these say is what the code does:

  • quilzo compliance summary counts, from the cryptographic inventory, the quantum-broken algorithms Quilzo generates and those it only verifies.
  • Each control and pledge statement says what is automatic and what happens only when somebody asks: a publication is timestamped and anchored by a command somebody runs, not on a schedule, and the hourly check of the binary is the admin server's, which the public site server does not run.
  • A FedRAMP 20x indicator whose related controls nothing checks reads stated in quilzo compliance ksi, not contributes.
  • quilzo posture scan --oscal keeps a risk accepted with posture suppress as not satisfied, with who accepted it, until when and why.
  • Backups are in the controls table as CP-9: what quilzo backup does, and what is left to you (see Back up and upgrade).
