This guide moved to Vaigil. Detections with their fixtures, Sigma rules, correlations, hunting, the ranked finding queue and the analyst agent are part of Vaigil, the security team's own tools, which began as this half of Quilzo. Its guide is Detections.

Quilzo keeps what it needs to defend itself, and hands the rest of the record to whoever watches it. Vaigil ships rules for Quilzo's own records (quilzo.*): a risky sign-in, or one its person reported, failures and lockouts, a decoy token touched, an administrator made or an administrator's token issued, a privileged change made alone, the audit log moved, break-glass, posture drifting, and the shield asking for a case. Quilzo's own response to an attack on itself is the shield, which acts without waiting for anybody.

Send Vaigil what happens here

Point the stream at Vaigil's feed, and quilzo serve pushes the audit log every fifteen seconds as signed OCSF batches that Vaigil can prove complete and unchanged:

quilzo config set siem.stream.url https://vaigil.example.org/feeds/quilzo
quilzo siem stream key --json > quilzo-stream-keys.json   # give these to Vaigil
quilzo siem stream status

People stay pseudonymous in what is sent unless siem.stream.reveal is set, which takes two administrators. See the audit log, streamed as it is written.
