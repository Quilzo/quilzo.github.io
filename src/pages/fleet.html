The fleet is Quilzo's inventory of AI: its own agents, connected apps, MCP tool servers, model routes, and other vendors' agents registered by their A2A cards, with a map of who called what drawn from the signed audit log.

Read the map

quilzo fleet --days 30

Register other vendors' agents

quilzo fleet add https://agents.example.com/.well-known/agent-card.json --name helpdesk-bot
quilzo fleet check
quilzo fleet remove helpdesk-bot

Quilzo reads the card when you register it and again on fleet check; a card that changed is a posture finding (fleet.card-changed).

Find shadow AI

An agent's own program is meant to reach models through the model gateway. When one reaches an AI service directly instead, its connection is in the log, and the Fleet screen lists it under shadow AI with the agent and the service. Lookalike hosts are not matched. What people across your organisation reach is in their proxy and DNS logs, which Vaigil reads.

quilzo fleet shadow --days 7

Direct use becomes the posture finding ai.shadow-use, and counts in the AI evidence.
