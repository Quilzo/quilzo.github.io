Making a picture, a video or a voice, and transcribing a recording, asks the store's models, so it needs the use-models permission, as the writing assistant does; storing what comes back needs edit-draft. --author names the person accountable for publishing it. Each goes through a route that does only that job: a video route for film, one that reads text aloud for speak, and one that transcribes for transcribe (see Models). A recording's voices cannot be masked, so it is sent only to a route that may receive personal data.

Transcription endpoints take a recording of at most 25 MB. A larger one is transcribed from its sound alone when ffmpeg is installed, and the draft is still timed as the video is.

Every upload is checked by what its bytes are, never by its name. A CSV file is refused when any cell begins as a formula would, wherever a spreadsheet would start a cell: on every line, after a comma, a semicolon or a tab, and after the byte-order mark.
