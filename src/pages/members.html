Turn accounts on with the setting site.members. Members sign in with passkeys, and no password. A recovery code, for a member who has lost their passkeys, opens the account to add a new passkey and nothing else, for fifteen minutes; adding it signs them in as a passkey sign-in would. Each code works once.

Members get the same lockout as the admin: ten failed sign-ins from places a member does not sign in from lock those places out of the account, and a hundred in a row turn its passkeys off until a recovery code is used. A locked-out sign-in gets the same answer as any failure, so it tells nobody that the account exists. The account page tells the member how many failed sign-ins came before their last one.
