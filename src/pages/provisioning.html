1. Issue the SCIM token

    quilzo scim token

    Give the identity provider the SCIM address and this token. It lasts 180 days; --days sets up to 365. quilzo scim status, the Provisioning screen and the posture check scim.token-expiring say when it ends, starting a month ahead. Make the next one before then: an ended token is refused, and that stops the identity provider taking access away too.

  2. Say what each group means

    quilzo scim map Editors author
quilzo scim map Security analyst
quilzo scim map "Lab technicians" lab-samples/technician

    A group means a role, a permission, a job, or a role an installed feature declares.

  3. Check it

    quilzo scim status

Only grants made by provisioning are ever changed by it; access granted by hand is left alone. Deactivation suspends a person at once: their sessions, tokens and connected apps stop, and agents they sponsor stop. Deletion also erases what agents remember about them. When a change that takes access away cannot be applied, Quilzo answers the identity provider with an error, keeps the person or group as they were, and the provider sends the request again; requests that arrive together are each applied in full.
