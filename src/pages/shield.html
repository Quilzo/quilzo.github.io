The shield is Quilzo protecting itself: playbooks that watch signals such as failed sign-ins, prompt injection against a chatbot or an agent following a planted instruction, and respond at once with protections that always end.

It is never turned on the inside: an administrator's own address keeps the admin, internal networks are never blocked, a lockdown nobody could get past is not applied, and the command line on the machine always works.
