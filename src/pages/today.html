Signing in opens Today, after Get started until you dismiss it. It has the same two parts every day, and nothing on it is written by a model.

Waiting for you

Everything you may decide, from every part of Quilzo: what agents asked, calls held for a second person, changes waiting for review, acts on other tools, held memories and features waiting to be installed. It lists the same items as Approvals, in this order:

  1. what was asked too long ago to agree to, so it stops holding its run;
  2. by what deciding commits to: questions, installs, then paying, deleting and publishing, which cannot be taken back, then sending, writing, acting and reading;
  3. the oldest first.

The first seven are shown, with a link to the rest on Approvals.

In your parts of the store

A count for each part of the store you can open, taken when the screen is loaded. Each count links to the records it counts:

CountShown to whoever can open
Pages changed in the draft and not published, among those you may readReview
Agent runs going now, finished in the last day, and failed, refused or stopped in the last dayAgents
Visitors waiting for a replyInbox
Findings to triageFindings
Cases openCases

The same grant check that decides the menu decides each count, so an analyst sees findings and cases and no pages, and a count is never shown for a screen you cannot open.

Where you start

Choose Start on Pages after signing in instead at the foot of Today to land on Pages, as before; Today stays in the menu, and Start here after signing in changes it back. The choice is kept in your browser, like the order of your menu.
