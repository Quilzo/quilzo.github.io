A visiting agent is an AI assistant acting for somebody looking at your site: a chat app answering their question, or the agent in their browser. Every Quilzo site offers such agents two things without any setting: it answers questions as data (NLWeb), and its forms say what they are for (WebMCP).

Asking the site

An agent asks in plain words at /ask and gets the published pages that match, each as a schema.org object:

curl 'https://your-site.example/ask?query=gluten-free+bread&streaming=false'
ParameterWhat it does
queryThe question or the words to look for; required, at most 300 characters.
modelist (the default) returns the matching pages. summarize and generate also return the answer your site's question box gives, with its sources and the AI disclosure, when it has one.
streamingServer-sent events by default; false for one JSON document.
decontextualized_queryThe question already put in context by the agent, answered in place of query.
query_idEchoed back; made up when not given.

Each result has its url, name, site, score, description and schema_object, the same structured data the page carries for search engines. The same is offered over MCP at /mcp on the public site, as one read-only tool called ask, so an agent that speaks MCP can connect to the site directly. This is not the admin's agent interface (see Connect AI apps), which acts for your own people and is not on the public site. /llms.txt tells agents where both are.

It answers only from what any visitor may read now: never a page for members, and nothing before or after its publish window. It changes nothing on the site. An answer in summarize or generate mode is the question box's own, with its limits and its refusal when your pages do not say.

Using its forms

WebMCP is a way for a page to say that a form is a tool: its name, what it does and what each field means. An agent in the visitor's browser then fills it in instead of guessing from a screenshot. Quilzo marks the forms itself, from what you declared:

FormThe agent may
Site search, and asking a chatbotRun it on its own: neither changes anything.
A declared form, such as an enquiry (see Forms)Fill it in; the visitor sends it. A form with a sensitive field is not offered.
A booking (see Bookings)Choose a free time and fill in the details; the visitor books it. Nothing is offered while bookings are paused.
Signing in, accounts and posting under a nameNothing: a person's own account and words are theirs to operate.

Every description comes from what you wrote: the form's label and notice, the fields' labels and help, the service's title, length and price. Browsers that do not know WebMCP ignore the markings, and no script is added to the page.

Who may ask

Both are the site's published content, so the rules in front of every page decide. When crawl.keys is set, a signed request from an AI crawler is held to your site's licence terms on /ask and /mcp exactly as on any page, and a question box answer is rate limited per address as it is on the site. To keep the site from being asked at all, unpublish what should not be read; there is nothing here that is not already public.
