Not part of Quilzo. Joining people and their machines across your tools, scoring their risk and reminding them to fix what is theirs belong to the identity and security tooling your organisation already runs. Quilzo knows only the people who use Quilzo.

Who may do what

Administration › People lists everybody with access and what each holds: roles on parts of the site, jobs that must end, permissions such as deciding one agent's runs, and denies, with their credentials, when each was last used and when it expires. quilzo auth list lists every grant, and quilzo auth explain WHO says why somebody can or cannot do a thing. Making somebody an administrator takes two administrators. See People and access.

How they sign in

Protection › Sign-ins shows every sign-in to the admin in the last thirty days: who, when, where from, the device, how risky it looked and why. Above them are the people waiting to confirm it is them, and the people locked out. quilzo token stale lists the credentials nobody has used for thirty days that still work, and quilzo token revoke ID ends one. See Sign-ins and Passkeys and tokens.

Kept in step with your identity provider

With SCIM, Okta, Microsoft Entra or another identity provider creates, updates, suspends and deletes people here, and their groups mean roles and jobs:

quilzo scim token
quilzo scim map Editors author
quilzo scim status

Deactivation suspends a person at once: their sessions, tokens and connected apps stop, and so do the agents they sponsor. Only grants made by provisioning are changed by it; access granted by hand is left alone. Administration › Provisioning shows the connection, what each group means and who was provisioned. See Provisioning.
