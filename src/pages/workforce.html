This guide moved to Vaigil. People and their machines joined across your tools, scored with reasons, and reminders sent to whoever can fix them are part of Vaigil, the security team's own tools, which began as this half of Quilzo. Its guide is Workforce risk.

Quilzo keeps what it needs to defend itself, and hands the rest of the record to whoever watches it. Who may do what in Quilzo is still Quilzo's: people and roles, provisioning from your identity provider, and the posture checks of how this deployment is set up.

Send Vaigil what happens here

Point the stream at Vaigil's feed, and quilzo serve pushes the audit log every fifteen seconds as signed OCSF batches that Vaigil can prove complete and unchanged:

quilzo config set siem.stream.url https://vaigil.example.org/feeds/quilzo
quilzo siem stream key --json > quilzo-stream-keys.json   # give these to Vaigil
quilzo siem stream status

People stay pseudonymous in what is sent unless siem.stream.reveal is set, which takes two administrators. See the audit log, streamed as it is written.
